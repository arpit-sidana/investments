# Employee-stock liquidation: tax-lot and wash-sale review method

**October 22, 2026. General research method.**

This report contains no personal account records, holdings, trade history or individualized sale quantities. A personal tax-lot recommendation requires account-specific evidence and should be kept with the underlying private records.

## Separate the cash requirement from the loss requirement

A sale's proceeds are not its taxable gain. For a specific lot:

`gain or loss = shares sold × execution price − adjusted basis allocated to those shares − applicable selling costs`

To raise cash while minimizing raw gains, compare the adjusted basis of each available lot with the expected execution price. At a fixed price and share count, the highest bases generate the largest raw losses. Holding period, wash-sale matching and the rest of the tax return can change which losses have the greatest tax value.

Do not assume that raising a particular dollar amount also realizes that amount of losses. Once every loss lot is sold, selling additional appreciated lots increases realized gains. Calculate the incremental loss and expected tax saving from each additional sale, rather than optimizing proceeds alone.

## Reconcile source completeness before choosing lots

Use a realized gain/loss export for past sales and a current-holdings export for unsold shares. They represent different populations.

For the realized export, check:

1. Export date range, account and symbol filters.
2. Detail-row count against any summary count, plus hidden rows, filters and extra sheets.
3. Shares, proceeds, adjusted basis and gains against summary totals.
4. Distinct execution orders versus individual tax-lot dispositions.
5. Original versus wash-adjusted bases, dates, gains and holding periods.

A display that shows only part of a table can coexist with a complete downloaded export. A detail-to-summary tie-out establishes completeness relative to that export's selection. It does not establish that the selection covers the full year. Compare the export with statements, confirmations, transferred-share history and eventual tax forms.

For current holdings, reconcile total shares multiplied by the snapshot price to position value. Preserve fractional precision and the time of the quote. Display-rounded basis can leave small differences from final tax accounting.

Broker estimates can omit adjustments needed on the tax return. [E*TRADE tax-record disclosure](https://us.etrade.com/l/f/disclosure-library/tax-records).

## Build the acquisition calendar

Under the wash-sale statute, a loss sale must be evaluated against relevant acquisitions within 30 calendar days before and after it. Include contracts and options to acquire substantially identical stock. Calendar days, trade dates and actual acquisition dates matter. [26 U.S.C. §1091](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-121/t26/s1091/).

Check payroll stock purchases, reinvested dividends, employee share awards and activity in other relevant accounts. A broker's adjustment display does not prevent future wash sales. Reporting within one brokerage account does not establish compliance across the taxpayer's investments. [E*TRADE wash-sale reporting](https://us.etrade.com/knowledge/library/taxes/wash-sale).

For a straightforward clean-window sale, identify the last actual relevant acquisition, wait at least 31 days, and prevent new relevant acquisitions for 30 days after the sale. Confirm that scheduled purchases can actually be stopped. An enrollment change may miss the next payroll cutoff or forgo matching compensation. Month-end labels should be reconciled to actual acquisition records. Include January activity when evaluating December sales.

## Model partial disallowance rather than assuming all or nothing

When replacement shares are fewer than sold loss shares, only matched losses may be deferred. Different lots can have different losses per share. The regulation applies losses in disposition order; when same-day disposition order cannot be determined, it uses acquisition chronology. Acquisition matching and already-used replacement shares also matter. [26 CFR §1.1091-1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/26/1.1091-1).

Normally, deferred losses increase a taxable replacement lot's basis and affect its holding period. A later disposition needs that adjusted basis. IRA replacement purchases have different consequences: a disallowed loss does not increase the IRA's basis. [IRS Publication 550](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550), [IRS Revenue Ruling 2008-5](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rr-08-05.pdf).

Do not promise that selling recent replacement shares in the same order produces no wash-sale reporting. Reconcile the sequence, the adjusted basis and any further acquisitions before relying on the final recognized loss.

## Verify employee-plan basis

An “ESPP” label alone does not determine the plan's tax treatment. Check its legal terms, payroll inclusion and the broker's supplemental stock-plan statement. For a qualifying statutory plan, ordinary income recognized on disposition may need to increase basis to prevent double taxation. [IRS stock-option and employee-purchase-plan guidance](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc427).

Some employer plans instead purchase at market value with separately taxable matching contributions. Use that plan's documents. Do not automatically add a generic discount or match percentage to the displayed basis. Reconcile compensation income exactly once.

## Combine accounts without double counting

Use all realized transactions and applicable capital-loss carryforwards. Distinguish executed sales from earlier proposals and unrealized losses. A rounded recollection and an exact brokerage summary may refer to the same gains, so reconcile them before adding.

Net short-term and long-term results separately and then apply the return's netting rules. The ordinary-income deduction limit for excess net capital losses is different from offsetting capital gains. Exact tax savings depend on the gains displaced, taxable income, residence and other return facts. [IRS Topic 409](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409).

NIIT may apply to the lesser of net investment income or MAGI above the filing-status threshold. Loss harvesting's marginal NIIT benefit requires the whole calculation. [IRS NIIT overview](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/net-investment-income-tax).

## Document execution and payment timing

Specify acquisition lots and quantities to the broker and retain written confirmation of the allocation. Confirm fees, fractional-order precision and the current execution price. Use a cash buffer when net proceeds must exceed a minimum. [IRS Publication 550](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550).

Recalculate if prices change or the sale date changes a lot's holding period. Verify the replacement investment separately before assuming it is not substantially identical.

Tax-year 2026 returns are generally due April 15, 2027, but estimated payments may be required earlier. Withholding, safe harbors and income timing affect the required payment. [IRS filing guidance](https://www.irs.gov/filing/individuals/how-to-file), [IRS Publication 505 (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505).

This is a dated research method, not a completed tax return. Verify current law and individual facts at execution and filing. Publication 550 currently displays its 2025 edition; the wash-sale statute and 2026 payment guidance were checked separately.
